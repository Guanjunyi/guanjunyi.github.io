About Me

I am an Associate Researcher at the School of Information Science and Technology, Hangzhou Normal University.

My research interests include data mining, unsupervised learning, clustering, pattern recognition, and superpixel segmentation. I am particularly interested in density-based clustering, multi-peak clustering, graph-based clustering, and hierarchical clustering for complex data distributions.
