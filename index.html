About Me

I am an Associate Researcher at the School of Information Science and Technology, Hangzhou Normal University.

My research interests lie in machine learning and pattern recognition, with a particular focus on clustering analysis, including complex-shaped clustering, density-based clustering, and cluster number estimation. I have published more than ten SCI-indexed papers as first or corresponding author in journals such as IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (TPAMI), IEEE Transactions on Image Processing (TIP), IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering (TKDE), IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems (TNNLS), Pattern Recognition, Information Sciences, and Neurocomputing.

I am currently leading projects supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC), including a General Program and a Young Scientists Fund project. I also enjoy working with students and collaborators on clustering, unsupervised learning, and related pattern recognition problems.
